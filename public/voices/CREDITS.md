# Enemy voices

Alert callouts: Kenney, **Voiceover Pack**, Creative Commons CC0.
Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/voiceover-pack
Included original license: Kenney-CC0.txt.

Pain and death reactions: Badre-Eddine, **Hurt / Death sound effect for character**, Creative Commons CC0.
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/hurt-death-sound-effect-for-character
Files from the author's download: 4, 5, 12, 16 (pain); 1, 3, 6, 9 (death).

CC0 terms: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Files remain unmodified. In-game playback varies pitch slightly and normalizes gain.

To replace these with your own performances, put audio files in this folder and
list their filenames under alert, pain, or death in manifest.json. Keep each clip
short (up to three seconds is played), and document permission for new assets.
